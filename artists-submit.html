Rhythmcore Records is an independent South African record label focused on helping upcoming artists develop their sound and pursue their musical goals.

We welcome artists across genres, from amapiano and hip-hop to afropop and sounds that don't fit into a single category.

You don't need to be famous to introduce yourself. Tell us who you are, share your music and let us discover what you bring to the scene.
