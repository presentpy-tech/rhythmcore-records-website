OUR STORY

Built from a passion for sound.

One vision. A growing movement. A sound of our own.

R R 2025

Rhythmcore Records began in 2025 with a vision: to create a platform where music, creativity and ambition could come together.

We believe a record label should be more than a name attached to a song. It should represent an identity, a commitment to creativity and a space where artists can work towards their goals.

Officially registered in 2026, Rhythmcore Records continues to build its foundation in South Africa. Our direction is open to different genres and sounds, from amapiano and hip-hop to Afropop and beyond.

Our focus is on discovering potential, developing original sounds and creating opportunities for upcoming artists to share their music with the world.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our purpose. Our direction.

The principles behind the Rhythmcore Records name.

01 / OUR MISSION

Develop the sound.

Our mission is to help upcoming artists develop their sound, express their identity and work towards their musical goals. We want to give original creativity a platform to grow.

02 / OUR VISION

Take the sound further.

Our vision is to grow Rhythmcore Records into a recognised independent label that connects artists and audiences through original music, creative ambition and a distinctive identity.

03 / OUR VALUES

Stay original.

Creativity, ambition, authenticity and a commitment to growth guide the way we approach music and the artists we work with.

OUR MUSICAL IDENTITY

No single genre.
No limits to creativity.

Music is always evolving, and we want Rhythmcore Records to evolve with it. Our interests span different genres, styles and creative directions.

From the rhythms of amapiano to the expression of hip-hop and the melodies of Afropop, we believe that every sound has the potential to tell a story.
